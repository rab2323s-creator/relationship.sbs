What this profile usually reflects

  • The relationship may be workable, but not on autopilot.
  • Several concerns need behavioral follow-through.
  • Hope is most useful when it can be tested against actions.
  • Repeated apologies without durable change should not be counted as full repair.

What this result does not mean

This profile does not determine whether you should stay or leave, diagnose either partner, or predict whether the relationship will last. It summarizes the pattern selected in this educational quiz.

Practical next steps

  1. Choose one or two measurable changes that matter most.
  2. Set a realistic reassessment point rather than extending the timeline indefinitely.
  3. Watch whether responsibility becomes more mutual after the request is clear.

Why the strongest two dimensions matter

Two people can receive the same overall Relationship Strain Score for very different reasons. One may be dealing mainly with a future mismatch; another may be dealing with failed repair and one-sided effort. Safety overrides the score

If the relationship involves fear, coercion, stalking, threats, forced sex, physical violence, financial control, or concern about leaving safely, do not use the overall score to minimize that. Seek appropriate private, real-world support.

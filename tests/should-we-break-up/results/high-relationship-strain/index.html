High Relationship Strain

Should We Break Up Quiz — Result Profile

Your answers suggest substantial difficulty across multiple relationship areas. You may be carrying high emotional cost while repair, trust, reciprocity, respect, or future fit are also under pressure. This is not an instruction to break up. It is a sign that the current pattern deserves serious attention.

What this profile usually reflects

  • Strain appears broad rather than limited to one small issue.
  • The relationship may require more adaptation than it provides restoration.
  • Repeated patterns matter more than isolated good or bad days.
  • Any safety concern should be treated separately from the overall score.

What this result does not mean

This profile does not determine whether you should stay or leave, diagnose either partner, or predict whether the relationship will last. It summarizes the pattern selected in this educational quiz.

Practical next steps

  1. Do not make a high-stakes decision in the middle of an escalating fight.
  2. Document repeated behaviors, attempted repairs, and what has or has not changed.
  3. If there is fear, coercion, stalking, threats, sexual pressure, or violence, prioritize private real-world safety support.

Why the strongest two dimensions matter

Two people can receive the same overall Relationship Strain Score for very different reasons. One may be dealing mainly with a future mismatch; another may be dealing with failed repair and one-sided effort. Safety overrides the score

If the relationship involves fear, coercion, stalking, threats, forced sex, physical violence, financial control, or concern about leaving safely, do not use the overall score to minimize that. Seek appropriate private, real-world support.

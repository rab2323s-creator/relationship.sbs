What this pattern can look like

  • There are real positive signals, but they do not fully agree.
  • Warmth may be stronger than follow-through.
  • The clearest next evidence is one meaningful behavior, not another tiny clue.

What to do next

  1. Stop collecting low-value signals for a while.
  2. If appropriate, make one direct low-pressure invitation or ask a simple question.
  3. Judge the response by what happens afterward, not only by the words.
What this result means

This result summarizes observable behavior from your answers. It does not prove what he privately feels, predict whether a relationship will happen, or identify hidden motives. Repeated behavior and direct communication remain stronger evidence than any quiz.

Low Observable Investment

Your answers suggest that warmth may exist, but most of the higher-value evidence—initiative, follow-through, effort, or progression—is limited. That does not prove he feels nothing. It means the behavior you reported is not currently building a strong case for active pursuit.

Mostly Friendly or Passive

Your answers suggest positive connection without enough direction to call the pattern clear romantic pursuit. He may be receptive, warm, attentive, or flirty while still leaving most of the initiation or progression to you.

Mixed Signals

Your answers contain enough positive evidence that purely friendly is too simple, but enough inconsistency that a confident yes would also be too simple. This is where chemistry can start outranking follow-through in your mind.

Consistent Interest, Cautious Pace

Your answers suggest more than friendliness: he appears to create contact, pay attention, invest effort, and maintain enough consistency that the connection has direction. One or two areas may still be slower, cautious, or less explicit.

Strong Observable Interest

Your answers describe a pattern in which his attention is not only warm but directional. He appears to create contact, remember and respond to you specifically, make or protect plans, and keep the connection moving after close moments.

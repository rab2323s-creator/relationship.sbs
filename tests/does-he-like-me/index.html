Does He Like Me? 15-Question Quiz: Is He Interested or Just Being Nice?

Stop counting emojis, eye contact, and one unusually good conversation. This quiz looks at the harder-to-fake pattern: does he initiate, pay attention, follow through, make time, and move the connection forward?

Answer based on the last several weeks, not your best moment together. The result measures observable behavior, not private thoughts.

The Night Maya Stopped Re-reading the Same Text

Maya had read the message eleven times.

“Had fun tonight :)”

There was a smiley face. There had been eye contact. He had laughed at the story about her disastrous driving test. He had touched her shoulder once when someone squeezed past them at the bar.

By midnight, those details had become a courtroom.

The smiley face was Exhibit A. The shoulder touch was Exhibit B. The fact that he had not suggested seeing her again was somehow both irrelevant and devastating.

Her friend asked one question that collapsed the whole case:

“Okay—but what is he actually doing to keep this going?”

Maya looked at the chat. She had started the last four conversations. She had suggested both meetups. When he canceled one, he apologized warmly but never offered another day.

The problem was not that there were no signs.

The problem was that she had been giving the smallest signs the largest meaning.

That is what this test is built to correct.

What This “Does He Like Me?” Quiz Actually Measures

There is no reliable quiz that can read another person's private feelings. What can be measured more honestly is the behavioral pattern around interest.

This test separates five dimensions:

Initiation

Does he create reasons to talk, reconnect, or see you—or mainly respond when you create the opportunity?

Attention & responsiveness

Does he engage with your actual thoughts, remember details, and show curiosity about your inner world?

Consistency & follow-through

Do warm moments survive the next day? Does a canceled plan become a new plan? Is his communication recognizable over time?

Investment & progression

Does he spend time, tolerate inconvenience, and help the connection move from chemistry into actual shared experiences?

Clarity vs general friendliness

Is his attention meaningfully different with you, or are you interpreting a generally warm personality as romantic direction?

Why Some Signs Matter More Than Others

Not all dating signals deserve equal weight.

Eye contact is easy. A compliment is easy. A fast reply can happen because someone is already holding their phone. Flirting can exist without any intention to build something.

Higher-value signals usually cost more: time, effort, planning, memory, inconvenience, emotional attention, and repeated follow-through.

That is why this quiz is weighted rather than treating every answer as equal. A question about rescheduling after a canceled plan carries more weight than a question about group attention. A question about whether the connection progresses carries more weight than one flirty interaction.

The total result is a 100-point observable-interest signal. It is not a probability that he likes you. It is a structured summary of the behavior you reported.

Does He Like Me or Is He Just Being Nice?

This is one of the hardest distinctions because genuine friendliness and early romantic interest share many surface behaviors: warmth, smiling, listening, joking, checking in, and being helpful.

The difference is often directionality.

Friendliness can stay broad and static. Romantic interest is more likely to become increasingly specific: more one-to-one contact, repeated initiation, remembering details, creating plans, following up after closeness, and making small sacrifices of convenience.

That does not mean every shy or cautious person will act boldly. It means that if attraction matters enough over time, some behavior usually begins to carry the connection forward.

What About Texting? Does He Like Me Over Text?

Texting can create false confidence because it produces lots of tiny data points.

A fast reply is weak evidence. A heart emoji is weak evidence. Even long conversations can be misleading if they never turn into initiative, plans, or progression.

More useful texting signals are:

  • he reopens conversations instead of only replying;
  • he asks follow-up questions rather than sending reaction-only messages;
  • he remembers earlier topics and circles back;
  • he moves from texting toward actual plans;
  • his warmth does not disappear immediately after a close or flirty moment.

Five Possible Result Profiles

  • Low Observable Investment — the behavioral evidence is thin right now.
  • Mostly Friendly or Passive — he may enjoy you without clearly pursuing more.
  • Mixed Signals — some behaviors point toward interest while others contradict them.
  • Consistent Interest, Cautious Pace — the connection has direction even if he is not especially bold.
  • Strong Observable Interest — initiative, effort, consistency, and progression repeatedly line up.

At the end, you also get your two strongest dimensions, so a score of 70 based mostly on attention will not be interpreted the same way as a 70 driven by investment and follow-through.

Why Mixed Signals Feel So Powerful

Uncertainty can make a connection more mentally consuming than a clear yes or a clear no.

When a person is warm, then distant, the brain has more unresolved information to process. People often start collecting increasingly small clues because the larger pattern is not settling the question.

That is exactly when the hierarchy of evidence matters most:

  1. Progression and investment
  2. Consistency and follow-through
  3. Initiation
  4. Specific attention and responsiveness
  5. Flirting, eye contact, response speed, and vibes

If the top of that list is weak, the bottom should not be forced to carry the whole conclusion.

When You Should Stop Taking Quizzes and Ask

There is a point where more analysis produces less clarity.

If the connection has had enough time to develop, you have real contact outside a purely professional context, and the ambiguity is beginning to cost you emotional energy, a simple direct move can give better information than another week of decoding.

You do not need a confession. You need one clear behavioral test:

“I’ve enjoyed talking with you. Want to grab coffee Thursday?”

The useful information is not only whether he says yes. It is whether he helps make the plan real.

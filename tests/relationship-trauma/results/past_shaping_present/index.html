What this pattern can look like

What to do next

  1. Consider a licensed trauma-informed clinician if the pattern is persistent or impairing.
  2. Track current evidence separately from trauma-linked predictions.
  3. Build relationships around consistency, accountability, and boundaries—not intensity.

Before you start

  • Answer based on what happens most often, not the ideal version of the relationship.
  • Use results as prompts for reflection, not as diagnoses or labels.
  • For safety-related patterns, prioritize observable behavior and your wellbeing over a score.

Educational self-reflection only. These tests do not diagnose mental-health conditions or determine whether another person has a disorder.

How results should be used

A result is a summary of the response pattern you selected in that test. It is designed to help you notice themes, compare behavior over time, and choose a practical next step.

It is not a clinical assessment, diagnosis, prediction, or verdict about you or another person. Relationship context matters, and one result should never outweigh direct evidence about safety, consent, respect, or repeated behavior.

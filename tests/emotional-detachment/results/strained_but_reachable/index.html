What this pattern can look like

  • You may share less because emotional effort feels expensive.
  • You still respond to evidence of care or change.
  • Space can feel good without making reunion feel unwanted.

What to do next

  1. Identify the one unresolved pattern most responsible for emotional fatigue.
  2. Create one repeatable connection ritual that is not about problem-solving.
  3. Check whether stress, sleep, caregiving, depression, or burnout is reducing emotional capacity more broadly.
Read the result carefully

This profile summarizes a response pattern. It is not a diagnosis, a validated clinical score, proof that you have stopped loving someone, or a recommendation to stay or leave.

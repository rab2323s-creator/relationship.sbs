What this pattern can look like

  • Your emotional distance may not be specific to your partner.
  • Several parts of life may feel less vivid than they used to.
  • Major relationship conclusions are harder to interpret when much of life feels emotionally muted.

What to do next

  1. Notice whether sleep, stress, mood, pleasure, motivation, and social connection changed together.
  2. Consider speaking with a qualified health or mental-health professional if numbness is persistent or distressing.
  3. Revisit the relationship question after you understand more about the broader emotional pattern.
Read the result carefully

This profile summarizes a response pattern. It is not a diagnosis, a validated clinical score, proof that you have stopped loving someone, or a recommendation to stay or leave.

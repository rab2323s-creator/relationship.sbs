What this profile usually reflects

  • Your wellbeing may be highly reactive to another person's emotional state.
  • Boundaries can feel like guilt, danger, or abandonment rather than ordinary limits.
  • Your own voice or identity may be difficult to access inside close relationships.

What this result does not mean

This profile does not diagnose codependency, dependent personality disorder, trauma, or any mental-health condition. It summarizes the pattern selected in this educational self-reflection test.

Practical next steps

  1. Start with one boundary around time, money, rescuing, or emotional availability.
  2. Rebuild separate identity through routines, relationships, and goals that are yours.
  3. Consider qualified support if the pattern is longstanding, difficult to interrupt, or linked to unsafe dynamics.

Why the strongest two dimensions matter

Two people can receive the same overall Codependency Pattern Score for different reasons. One may mainly struggle with approval-linked self-worth; another may score highest in rescuing and self-neglect.

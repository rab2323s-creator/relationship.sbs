Approval-Linked Caretaking

Codependency Test — Result Profile

Your answers suggest that helping may sometimes serve two jobs at once: supporting someone else and reassuring you that you are good, lovable, or necessary.

Take the full test Browse all relationship tests

What this profile usually reflects

  • Disapproval may quickly become self-doubt.
  • Being needed can feel closely tied to being valuable.
  • You may edit needs or opinions to protect closeness.

What this result does not mean

This profile does not diagnose codependency, dependent personality disorder, trauma, or any mental-health condition. It summarizes the pattern selected in this educational self-reflection test.

Practical next steps

  1. Separate 'Are they happy with me?' from 'Was my boundary fair?'
  2. Practice one preference per day that is not optimized around another person's reaction.
  3. Build sources of self-worth that do not depend on being indispensable.

Why the strongest two dimensions matter

Two people can receive the same overall Codependency Pattern Score for different reasons. One may mainly struggle with approval-linked self-worth; another may score highest in rescuing and self-neglect.

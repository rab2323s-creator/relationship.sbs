What this pattern can look like

  • You share in layers rather than all at once.
  • You may feel a vulnerability hangover after opening up.
  • You prefer to handle some needs independently.
  • Closeness grows when the other person proves reliable.

What to do next

  1. Practice one small disclosure beyond your usual safe script.
  2. Ask directly for one manageable form of support.
  3. Notice whether caution changes with evidence or stays rigid no matter what.
Do not overread the label

This profile describes the pattern selected in your answers. It is not the original Fear-of-Intimacy Scale, a clinical diagnosis, proof of an attachment style, or evidence about another person's motives.

What this pattern can look like

  • You can usually say what you feel without excessive shame.
  • Receiving care does not automatically feel like weakness or debt.
  • You can ask for space without emotionally disappearing.
  • Commitment and autonomy can coexist.

What to do next

  1. Keep choosing relationships where openness is met with respect and reciprocity.
  2. Protect boundaries even when connection feels easy.
  3. Watch whether the other person can tolerate the same honesty you offer.
Do not overread the label

This profile describes the pattern selected in your answers. It is not the original Fear-of-Intimacy Scale, a clinical diagnosis, proof of an attachment style, or evidence about another person's motives.

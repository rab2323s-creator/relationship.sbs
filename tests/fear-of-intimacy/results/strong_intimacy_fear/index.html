Strong Intimacy-Fear Pattern

Fear of Intimacy Test — result profile

Your answers suggest that intimacy frequently activates a strong protective response across more than one area: being known, depending on someone, allowing commitment, staying connected after vulnerability, or tolerating emotionally meaningful physical closeness. This does not mean you are incapable of love. It means closeness may currently carry enough threat that distance feels safer.

What this pattern can look like

  • Emotional exposure may quickly trigger regret, shutdown, or escape.
  • Depending on someone can feel like giving away power.
  • Distance may bring immediate relief even when it creates loneliness later.
  • Safe people may still activate protection before there is clear evidence of danger.

What to do next

  1. Identify the strongest trigger rather than trying to fix everything at once.
  2. Practice gradual, consent-based closeness rather than flooding yourself.
  3. If the pattern is persistent, painful, or connected with trauma or panic, consider qualified professional support.
Do not overread the label

This profile describes the pattern selected in your answers. It is not the original Fear-of-Intimacy Scale, a clinical diagnosis, proof of an attachment style, or evidence about another person's motives.

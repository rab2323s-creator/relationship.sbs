Dating App Addiction: Why Can't You Stop Swiping?

She had forty-two matches and nobody she wanted to telephone. The strange loneliness of an entire city inside a pocket.

Published Oct 9, 2026Research-informed18 min read

Quick answer:

Dating app addiction is a popular phrase for compulsive dating app use: repeatedly swiping, checking matches or chasing conversations even when you intended to stop or the habit is interfering with your life. It is not an established standalone psychiatric diagnosis. Nine signs include automatic checking, swiping without enjoyment, validation-seeking, avoiding actual meetings, endless comparison, seeking novelty over connection, repeated deleting and returning, neglected priorities, and losing sight of what you want.

Are AI Girlfriend Chats Private? Who Can Read, Store or Delete Them?

Are AI girlfriend chats private? Your messages might be hidden from other users, but that is not the same as being inaccessible to the company running the service. If you have told an AI girlfriend, boyfriend, or romantic character about your relationship, your sexuality, or a personal secret, you deserve a precise answer—not a vague promise of “private chats.” This guide checks the documented controls at Replika, Character.AI, and Nomi: who can see a conversation, how providers process it, and what you can actually delete. It also explains what to do if you have already shared something sensitive.

Are AI girlfriend chats private? The short answer

AI companion chats can be private from other users while still being processed by the company operating the app. The service needs your input to generate replies and may use stored information for continuity, personalization, safety, support, or other purposes disclosed in its policy. Different companies place different limits on those uses.

For example, Character.AI says the people who create characters cannot see your private conversations with those characters. That is reassuring about one particular audience; it does not mean Character.AI itself never processes your messages. Replika says other people do not join your conversations, and its policy says chat content is not shared with advertisers. Yet its service uses third-party AI model providers to generate replies and limited anonymized excerpts for internal safety improvements. Nomi discourages sharing personally identifying information and discloses retention exceptions for certain archives.

The three questions to ask: Who can see my chat? What will the service do with its contents? What remains after I delete a chat, memory, or account? These are separate questions, with separate answers.

Who can actually see your AI girlfriend conversations?

The word “private” hides four different audiences. An AI companion may be private from strangers without providing the same protection against the service itself, its infrastructure providers, or anyone who gains access to your account or phone.

  • Other users and character creators: Character.AI states that the person who created a public Character cannot read your conversations with it. Replika says no human joins its chats or replies pretending to be the AI. These statements concern what other people can ordinarily do in the product; they are not independent audits of every internal system.
  • The platform operator: a hosted companion service receives and processes messages to generate replies. Policies may also describe storage, personalization, safety processing, technical support, and legally required disclosures. Do not interpret “only you see the chat history” as “the company receives no chat data.”
  • AI model and service providers: Replika expressly describes sending minimized or de-identified conversation data to third-party model providers to generate responses, with contractual restrictions against those providers training on it. This is not the same as a promise that no third-party processor is involved.
  • People with your own access: a partner, family member, or stranger who can open an unlocked device or account may see saved messages. Privacy settings for a character do not substitute for device locks, strong authentication, and control over shared screenshots.

Bottom line: Character.AI's answer to “Can the character creator see this?” is no. The broader question “Can the company process this?” requires reading its data policy and settings. Those two answers are not contradictory.

How to delete Replika chat history—and why clearing memory is different

If your search is “how to delete Replika conversations,” the key limitation is explicit: Replika's current help page says you cannot delete your entire conversation history while keeping the account. To remove the full chat history using the documented account controls, you must delete the account. This is irreversible and also affects the companion and associated content.

Delete a Replika account

  1. On mobile, open Replika, enter Settings, find Account Settings, and choose Delete Replika or the deletion option shown in your version.
  2. On the web, sign in at my.replika.com, open Account Settings, and select Delete my Account.
  3. Follow the confirmation flow, including any password request. Check any paid subscription separately so you understand upcoming charges.

The help center uses slightly different menu labels across its articles and app versions, so follow the label visible in your own settings. Deleting and recreating an account is not a reversible “reset” of an existing Replika.

What deleting Replika memories does—and does not—do

In its Memory area, you can inspect or remove saved details. This may change how the companion personalizes future responses, but removing a memory is not proof that the original conversation text has been erased. Similarly, changing profile details will not necessarily erase information you already disclosed in a chat.

Can Replika use my chats to train AI?

Replika's current privacy policy says third-party language-model providers are contractually prohibited from training their own models on Replika's conversation data, which is sent to generate responses under data-minimization measures. It also says limited, immediately anonymized excerpts may be used internally to improve safety systems and chatbot performance. Avoid the inaccurate blanket statement that chats are either “never used for training” or “always used for training”: the policy distinguishes uses.

The same policy discusses account-related deletion requests and retention exceptions. It lists profile data, messages, and preferences processed for contractual purposes for up to 60 days after termination, and some financial records for longer legal periods. The app's “permanently removed” help wording should be understood alongside those details.

Is Character.AI private? Export, deletion, and training controls

Character.AI gives two distinct answers that people often mix together. Its help center says character creators cannot see the conversations you have with their characters. Separately, its privacy documentation describes using data, including some user-generated content, to operate and improve the service and its models. Privacy from another creator does not mean the platform has no data-processing role.

How to export Character.AI chats and account data

  1. On the website: go to your Profile → Profile Settings → Account → Manage Account & Data → Export Data.
  2. In the mobile app: go to your Profile → Settings → Legal & Privacy → Export Data.
  3. Wait for the email with the download link, then download the archive securely.

As of the help article updated October 8, 2026, Character.AI says exports can take three to four weeks to process and the emailed download link is valid for seven days. If you want an archive, request it before deleting the account and do not assume you can obtain it immediately.

How to delete a Character.AI account

On web, use Profile → Profile Settings → Account → Manage Account & Data → Delete Account. On mobile, the help center directs users to their profile settings and Remove Account. The deletion is permanent; if your goal is to recover a conversation later, create your export request first.

Opt out of using Character.AI chats for training

Character.AI's published instructions specifically address users in the EEA and UK. In the app, open Settings → Data & Privacy and switch off Improve the Model for Everyone. On web, go to Settings → Account → Manage Account & Data → Model Improvement and deselect that option. The company says the choice applies to new content; other service improvement and safety processing may still occur. Do not assume this choice retroactively removes earlier training material.

Character.AI also offers a “Rewind to Here” feature for removing subsequent messages from a conversation's visible path. That is a conversation-editing feature, not documented proof of full backend erasure. If legal deletion rights matter to you, use the account or privacy-request channel rather than relying on a chat-interface action.

Does Nomi save chats? What its data-deletion policy actually promises

Nomi's privacy policy describes a service designed to limit unnecessary identifying information. It recommends that you avoid entering personally identifiable information in interactions and customization. That recommendation matters even if you trust a chatbot emotionally: a service can minimize what it asks for, yet still receive identifying facts you volunteer.

To delete your Nomi account, the company's official deletion page says to sign in, open Account Settings, choose Delete Account, and follow the instructions. That help page says associated Nomis and messages are permanently deleted. However, its privacy policy is more specific about timing and exceptions.

The policy, effective April 27, 2026, describes deletion of personal information within “28 or so days” after confirmation, while noting exceptions including training or communications archives and information required for legal proceedings. It says information in training archives would no longer be attributable to you after deletion. This is a stated company policy, not independent verification that every copy everywhere disappears on a particular day.

What about exporting a full Nomi conversation? We found rights-to-access and data-transfer language in the privacy policy, but not a clearly documented, universal one-click chat-export sequence in the official material reviewed. If an archive matters to you, ask support@nomi.ai what is available for your account and region before you delete it.

Verify: Nomi privacy policy (especially sections 4, 6, and 7) and account-deletion instructions.

Deleting chats, deleting memories, deleting an account: four different actions

A common privacy mistake is to use these terms as if they were interchangeable. Consider the following sequence: you share a personal story, the bot saves a summary as a “memory,” you remove that memory, and later you close the account. Each step can affect a different layer of data.

  • Hiding, archiving, rewinding, or editing a chat: changes what appears in the interface. Unless the service says otherwise, it should not be treated as proof every stored copy was removed.
  • Deleting a memory: may remove a stored fact from the companion's personalization tools. It does not automatically establish that original messages or logs were deleted.
  • Opting out of model training: changes an allowed use of data. It does not necessarily close your account, erase chat history, or prevent processing necessary for the service or safety.
  • Deleting an account or exercising a data-erasure right: is the broadest request, but the provider's legal retention rules, backend schedules, and exceptions still matter.

Practical example: if your goal is simply “I don't want my AI companion to bring up my ex,” a memory or personalization change may be enough. If your goal is “I want the provider to stop holding information about my past relationship,” the relevant question is the scope of an account-deletion or privacy request. Those are not the same request.

Already shared an intimate secret? What to do next

If you have sent romantic roleplay, deeply personal stories, photos, or details about a real partner to an AI companion, there are practical steps you can take without assuming the worst or giving the service more information. Choose the situation that matches your concern.

“I told my AI girlfriend something embarrassing about myself.”

First, avoid adding identifying details or asking the chatbot to repeat the secret. Review its memory and conversation controls; the information saved as an editable memory may be different from the transcript. If you want all data erased rather than merely hidden, use the provider's documented deletion or privacy-request process, and ask about the scope of retained copies.

“I uploaded an intimate photo or information about my partner.”

Stop sending additional copies. Consider whether another person's consent or confidentiality has been affected—especially for identifiable photos, messages, health information, or sexual details. A chat appearing private does not grant permission to disclose someone else's life. For shared relationship messages, our guide to AI and couple-message privacy addresses consent and boundaries separately.

“I want to leave the service, but I may need my chat history.”

Request a data export before account deletion when one is available. Character.AI currently estimates three to four weeks to prepare exports and provides a link valid for seven days. If you have an active subscription, cancel billing separately through the relevant payment channel. Keep a dated confirmation of deletion, then review saved exports and screenshots on your devices as well.

“I want privacy without deleting my AI companion.”

Remove optional identifiers, reduce what you share, use memory controls, and check any training settings available to your account and location. Character.AI's documented EEA/UK training opt-out applies to new content; it is not a general erase button. Use a separate email if that suits your circumstances, but understand that an alias does not make disclosed intimate details disappear.

A useful rule: if you would hesitate to send the detail to a company-operated online service, do not assume romantic language or a lifelike avatar changes the data flow. You can enjoy an AI companion while setting clear boundaries about what remains offline.

AI girlfriend chat privacy: frequently asked questions

Can my real-life partner read my AI girlfriend conversations?

Not merely because you use an AI companion. Access usually depends on whether you share transcripts or someone can get into your device or account. Review shared-device access and account security, and avoid posting screenshots containing other people's personal information.

Does deleting a chat stop a company using it for training?

Not necessarily. Hiding a conversation, deleting an account, and limiting training are separate controls. A company's policy and the specific deletion request determine the outcome; do not assume editing the visible chat rewrites previously processed data.

Are Replika conversations private?

Replika says chats are private and not shared with advertisers, while its privacy policy describes processing to provide responses, safety functions, and retention obligations. Read “private” as a scoped claim, not a promise that the provider never handles chat content.

Can I delete Replika messages without deleting my account?

According to its current official help, not the entire conversation history. The documented way to remove it fully is account deletion. A saved memory can be removed separately but is not the same thing as deleting all chats.

Can Character.AI creators read my chats?

Its official help center says no. The platform's own data processing is a different issue; the privacy policy and model-training settings explain those controls.

How long does a Character.AI data export take?

The help article updated October 8, 2026 says approximately three to four weeks, with a download link valid for seven days once emailed. Submit your request before deleting your account.

Does deleting Nomi erase everything immediately?

Nomi's public account-deletion page describes permanent deletion of account data and messages. Its privacy policy discusses roughly 28 days and exceptions for certain archives and legal needs. These statements should be read together, not selectively.

Are AI girlfriend apps end-to-end encrypted?

Do not assume so from generic “secure chat” marketing. Look for a precise product statement defining the encryption model, who controls the keys, and what the provider itself can access. Encryption in transit alone is not equivalent to end-to-end encryption.

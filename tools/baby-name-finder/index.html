THE THOUGHTFUL GUIDE

How to Find the Perfect Baby Name

A name can connect a child to family, language and culture. These practical steps can help you decide together.

01

Begin with what matters

Do you love a sound, a family connection or a particular meaning? Share those priorities before comparing lists. It is easier to agree on a direction than on a name right away.

02

Consider how it travels

Say each name aloud in the languages your family uses. Ask how others might pronounce or spell it, and check whether the name works comfortably with your surname.

03

Give each other room

Make shortlists independently, then look for overlaps. A name one partner dislikes need not become an argument; explore what the other partner loves about it.

Search Baby Names by Letter, Gender and Style

Start with the search box to look for a name or a word in an available meaning. Choose Girls, Boys or Both, then open More filters to browse by first letter, name length or meaning. Sort A–Z or Z–A to scan the collection at your own pace. The same name may be used in different ways across languages and cultures.

What do baby name meanings and origins actually tell you?

Meanings are short explanations of a name’s linguistic history. Some names have more than one possible root, especially when they appear across languages or share a spelling. This finder includes 20,000 baby-name spellings, with documented meanings and language roots shown where available. A missing meaning does not make a name less meaningful to your family.

Names that share a sound or spelling may have different histories. Treat meaning tags as starting points for discovery, not predictions about personality, and think about how a name is pronounced in the languages used at home.

Choose Baby Names Together: Compare Your Favorites

Try making separate shortlists first: each parent saves around ten names. Share your favorites, look for names on both lists and talk about the sounds, meanings or family connections you each love. If there is no overlap, compare qualities such as short versus long or classic versus modern rather than trying to win support for a single favorite.

You can also revisit your thoughts about parenthood and explore other relationship guides. Choosing a name is one of many meaningful family decisions you can make as a team.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Baby Name Finder FAQs

Is this baby name finder free?

Yes. You can explore the names, save favorites and share a shortlist without creating an account or paying a fee.

Can I search baby names by starting letter?

Yes. Open More filters and choose the first letter you want. You can combine this with the name search and the girl, boy or gender-neutral options.

Can I use it with my spouse or partner?

Yes. Save your favorite names, copy a shortlist link and send it to your partner. When they open your link, they can save their own picks and see which names overlap.

Do all 20,000 names have a verified meaning?

No. Documented meanings and language roots are available for only some names. Others remain searchable and can be saved to your favorites. Meaning filters include names with available documentation.

Are the name meanings always definitive?

No. Etymological explanations may differ by language, source and naming tradition. Treat the glosses as a starting point, and verify a final choice using the cited dictionary source and speakers familiar with the language.

Where are my saved names stored?

Your favorites are saved locally in your browser when storage is available. A share link contains only the selected names in its address. Anyone who receives the link can read those names. We do not store a shared shortlist on a server.

What's a good name for a boy?

A good boy name is one that suits your family's languages, surname and preferences. Liam, Noah and Oliver are examples to consider. You can also compare shorter options or documented meanings in the Baby Name Finder.

What is a cool name for a boy?

Cool is a matter of taste. Kai, Milo, Axel and Finn are short options you might like. Search for them in the tool, then explore more boy names by letter, length or meaning.

What are beautiful baby girl names?

Olivia, Aurora, Violet and Lily are examples of girl names that some families find beautiful. Browse the girl names collection to compare their sounds, spellings and documented meanings where available.

What are classic girl names?

Charlotte, Eleanor, Alice and Clara are examples of classic-style girl names. What feels traditional depends on your language and culture, so use the finder to compare names with your own family's preferences.

How do I find baby names that start with A?

Use the Girl Names or Boy Names A–Z letter guide above and select A. It opens the search tool with the matching category and first-letter filter. You can do the same for every letter through Z.

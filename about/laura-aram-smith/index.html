Laura Aram Smith, Psychologist

Laura Aram Smith leads the editorial team at relationship.sbs. She writes and reviews educational content about relationship communication, attachment patterns, boundaries, emotional safety, and conflict repair.

The site publishes practical relationship tools, self-reflection tests, scripts, and guides. Its content is educational and is not a substitute for diagnosis, therapy, medical care, legal advice, or emergency support.

Topics covered

  • Attachment and relationship patterns
  • Communication and conflict repair
  • Healthy boundaries and emotional safety
  • Relationship anxiety and overthinking
  • Emotional availability and intimacy

Editorial approach

Content is written for clarity, practical use, and context. relationship.sbs avoids presenting self-reflection tools as diagnoses and updates content when corrections or stronger evidence are needed.
